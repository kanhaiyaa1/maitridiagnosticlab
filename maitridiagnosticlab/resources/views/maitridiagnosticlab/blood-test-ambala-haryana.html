Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Blood Testing Services

Is fasting required for blood tests at Maitri Diagnostic Lab?
Fasting requirements depend on the specific test you're taking. For tests like fasting blood sugar, lipid profile (cholesterol test), and liver function tests, you typically need to fast for 8-12 hours before the blood sample collection. However, for routine tests like Complete Blood Count (CBC), thyroid function tests (TSH, T3, T4), kidney function tests, or vitamin tests, fasting is usually not necessary. When you book your appointment, our medical team will clearly inform you about any specific preparation requirements including fasting instructions for your particular tests.
How long does it take to get blood test reports from Maitri Diagnostic Lab in Ambala?
Most routine blood test reports are available within 6-24 hours of sample collection. Common tests like CBC, blood sugar, thyroid profile, kidney and liver function tests typically have same-day or next-day results. Some specialized tests such as hormone panels, cancer markers, or culture tests may take 2-3 days for complete analysis. For urgent cases, we offer express testing with reports available within a few hours. You can access your reports through multiple channels - online via our website, email, WhatsApp, or collect hard copies from our Ambala center. We also provide free doctor consultation for report interpretation if needed.
