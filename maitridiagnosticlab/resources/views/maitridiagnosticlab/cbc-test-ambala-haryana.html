What is CBC Test?

CBC (Complete Blood Count) is one of the most common blood tests that measures various components of your blood including red blood cells, white blood cells, hemoglobin, hematocrit, and platelets. This essential diagnostic test helps doctors detect a wide range of health conditions from anemia to infections.

Why Get CBC Test in Ambala?

  • Diagnosing anemia, infections, and blood disorders
  • Monitoring existing medical conditions
  • Pre-surgery health evaluation
  • Assessing overall health status
  • Detecting vitamin deficiencies
