What Changes in Your Body After 30?

After crossing 30 years of age, several physiological changes occur that make regular health monitoring essential:

30-35 Years
Metabolism Slows Down

Your metabolic rate decreases by 2-3% per decade, increasing risk of weight gain and diabetes. Regular blood sugar monitoring becomes important for Ambala residents.

35-40 Years
Bone Density Declines

Especially in women, bone mass starts declining. Vitamin D and Calcium monitoring is crucial in Haryana's climate.

40+ Years
Cardiovascular Risk Increases

Cholesterol levels and blood pressure need regular monitoring. Heart disease risk factors become more prevalent.
