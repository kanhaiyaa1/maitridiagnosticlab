The Liver Function Test (LFT), also known as hepatic panel or liver panel, is a group of blood tests that measure various enzymes, proteins, and substances produced or processed by the liver. What's Included in LFT Test?

Complete Liver Panel with Key Parameters

SGPT (ALT) - Alanine Aminotransferase

Enzyme primarily found in the liver. Elevated levels indicate liver cell damage or inflammation.

SGOT (AST) - Aspartate Aminotransferase

Enzyme found in liver and other organs. Helps assess liver damage severity.

Total Bilirubin

Yellow pigment produced during breakdown of red blood cells. High levels cause jaundice.

Direct Bilirubin

Bilirubin processed by the liver. Helps differentiate types of jaundice.

Indirect Bilirubin

Unconjugated bilirubin not yet processed by liver.

Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)

Enzyme in liver and bile ducts. Elevated in bile duct obstruction.

Total Protein

Measures all proteins in blood. Reflects liver's protein production ability.

Albumin

Main protein made by liver. Low levels indicate chronic liver disease.

Globulin

Group of proteins including antibodies. Calculated from total protein minus albumin.

A/G Ratio

Albumin to Globulin ratio. Helps assess liver and kidney function.

LFT Test Preparation & Fasting Guidelines

Fasting Requirements for LFT Test

8-12 hours of fasting is recommended before your LFT blood test for the most accurate results. This means you should not eat or drink anything except water for 8-12 hours before sample collection.

  • Water: You can drink plain water during the fasting period
  • No Food: Avoid all solid foods, snacks, and meals
  • No Beverages: Avoid tea, coffee, juice, milk, and other drinks
  • Timing: Best time for sample collection is early morning (7-10 AM)
  • Medications: Continue regular medications unless your doctor advises otherwise

Preparation Tips

  • Avoid alcohol consumption for at least 24 hours before the test
  • Avoid strenuous exercise on the day of the test
  • Inform your doctor about all medications you're taking
  • Get adequate rest the night before the test
  • Wear comfortable clothing with easy arm access for blood draw
  • Bring your prescription or previous reports if available
