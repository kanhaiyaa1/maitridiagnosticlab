What Causes High Blood Sugar?

Understanding the causes of high blood sugar helps in prevention. Common causes observed in Ambala patients include:

  • Type 1 Diabetes: Pancreas doesn't produce insulin (autoimmune condition)
  • Type 2 Diabetes: Body becomes resistant to insulin (most common type in Ambala)
  • Poor Diet: High intake of sugary foods, refined carbs, and processed foods
  • Lack of Exercise: Sedentary lifestyle common in urban Ambala
  • Stress: Increases cortisol which raises blood sugar
  • Medications: Steroids and certain other drugs
  • Illness or Infection: Can temporarily raise blood sugar
  • Family History: Genetic predisposition to diabetes

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about blood sugar levels in Ambala, Haryana

What is a normal blood sugar level?

Normal fasting blood sugar levels are between 70-100 mg/dL. Post-meal (2 hours after eating), normal levels should be less than 140 mg/dL. HbA1c levels below 5.7% are considered normal. At Maitri Diagnostic Lab in Ambala, Haryana, we provide accurate blood sugar testing with detailed reports to help you understand your glucose levels and take appropriate action.

What is the difference between fasting and PP blood sugar?

Fasting blood sugar (FBS) is measured after 8-12 hours of not eating, typically in the morning before breakfast. PP (Post-Prandial) blood sugar is measured exactly 2 hours after you start eating a meal. Both tests are important for diagnosing and monitoring diabetes. Fasting sugar shows how well your body manages glucose overnight, while PP shows how your body handles food. Maitri Diagnostic Lab in Ambala offers both tests with home sample collection service for your convenience.

What is HbA1c and why is it important?

HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin or A1c) shows your average blood sugar levels over the past 2-3 months. It's the gold standard test for diabetes diagnosis and management because it gives a complete picture rather than a single point in time. Normal HbA1c is below 5.7%, prediabetes is 5.7-6.4%, and diabetes is 6.5% or higher. Unlike fasting sugar, HbA1c doesn't require fasting. Get your HbA1c tested at Maitri Diagnostic Lab in Ambala, Haryana for accurate diabetes screening.

How often should I check my blood sugar levels in Ambala?

For healthy adults in Ambala, annual blood sugar screening is recommended after age 30. If you have prediabetes or risk factors (family history, overweight, sedentary lifestyle), test every 3-6 months. Diabetics should test as advised by their doctor - often daily for fasting levels and HbA1c every 3 months. Maitri Diagnostic Lab offers convenient blood sugar testing packages in Ambala with home collection, making regular monitoring easy.

What causes high blood sugar levels?

High blood sugar can be caused by insufficient insulin production (Type 1 diabetes), insulin resistance (Type 2 diabetes), poor diet high in sugar and refined carbs, lack of exercise, stress, illness, obesity, or certain medications like steroids. In Ambala and Haryana, lifestyle factors such as sedentary work, high-carb diets, and family history play significant roles. What should I do if my blood sugar is high?

If your blood sugar is high, consult a doctor immediately, especially if above 200 mg/dL. Get proper confirmatory testing done at an -accredited lab like Maitri Diagnostic Lab in Ambala. Follow medical advice, maintain a healthy low-sugar diet, exercise regularly for 30 minutes daily, manage stress, lose weight if overweight, and monitor your levels regularly. Is fasting required for blood sugar test?

For fasting blood sugar (FBS) test, you need to fast for 8-12 hours (only water allowed). For HbA1c, no fasting is required. For post-prandial (PP) sugar, you eat normally and test exactly 2 hours after starting your meal. Random blood sugar doesn't require fasting.
