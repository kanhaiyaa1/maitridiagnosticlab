Understanding Thyroid Tests in Ambala, Haryana

Your thyroid gland plays a crucial role in regulating your metabolism, energy levels, and overall health. At Maitri Diagnostic Lab in Ambala, Haryana, we understand the importance of timely thyroid testing for residents of Ambala, Haryana, and surrounding areas.

A thyroid test measures the levels of thyroid hormones in your blood, including TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone), T3 (Triiodothyronine), and T4 (Thyroxine). These tests help diagnose conditions like hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, and other thyroid disorders that are increasingly common in Haryana.

When Should You Get Your Thyroid Tested?

Many people in Ambala and Haryana struggle with undiagnosed thyroid issues. Here are the key situations when you should consider getting a thyroid test at our diagnostic center:

Unexplained Weight Changes

Sudden weight gain or loss without diet changes

Constant Fatigue

Feeling tired despite adequate sleep

Hair Loss

Excessive hair thinning or hair fall

Mood Changes

Depression, anxiety, or irritability

Temperature Sensitivity

Always feeling too hot or too cold

Irregular Periods

Menstrual cycle changes in women

Heart Palpitations

Rapid or irregular heartbeat

Family History

Thyroid disorders run in your family
